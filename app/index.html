Loan Decision Engine

Automated loan risk assessment and decision routing. Submit a loan application to receive a risk score and determine the appropriate next step.

Decision Workflow

  1. Score Risk — The ML model evaluates the application and generates a risk score.
  2. Route Application — Based on the risk score, the application is automatically routed:
    • Low Risk — Approve: an approval response is generated automatically.
    • Medium Risk — Review: the application is escalated to a human underwriter for review.
    • High Risk — Decline: a decline response is generated automatically.
  3. Generate Response — For automated decisions, an AI-generated customer response explains the outcome.
  4. Validate Response — The generated response is checked for potentially biased or inappropriate language before delivery.
  5. Send Response — Validated responses are ready to be sent to the customer.
